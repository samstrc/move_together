Move Together

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Move Together is a student project by The Relational Rebels (Sam, Caius, and Qiaozhi) for a university Databases course. It helps people who are moving in together plan a shared list and budget. This page explains what the app (the website and the iOS app) stores and why.

What we store

  • Your account: your name, email address, and password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash (Argon2), never as plain text.
  • Your group's data: the shared list, budgets, expenses, who paid and who owes what, invite codes, and group details such as the move date and new address you choose to enter.
  • Chats with Dolly: the messages you send to Dolly, her replies, and the products she shows you.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers, and the app has no ads or analytics trackers.

How it's used

  • To run the app: signing you in and showing your group's list, budget, and chats to the members of that group.
  • To answer your questions to Dolly. Your message, along with your group's list and budget details Dolly needs, is sent to OpenAI to generate a reply. OpenAI's handling of that data is covered by its privacy policy.
  • When Dolly finds products, the app opens those store pages to check they're still for sale and to get their pictures and prices. Stores don't receive any of your information from this.

We don't sell your data or share it with anyone else.

Where it's stored

Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Render in the United States. The app keeps you signed in with a login token saved on your device, which expires after a day.

Who can see your data

Only the members of a group can see that group's list, budget, and expenses. Your chats with Dolly are visible only to you. The project team can access the database to run and fix the app.

Deleting your data

You can remove items, expenses, and members, leave a group, or delete a group you own at any time in the app. To have your account and all of its data deleted, contact us at the address below.

Children

Move Together isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/samstrc/move_together/issues, or send feedback through TestFlight if you're testing the iOS app.
